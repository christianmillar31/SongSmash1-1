# SongSmash Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 9, 2026**

SongSmash is a music party game for iOS. This policy explains what
information the app handles. The short version: **SongSmash collects no
personal data at all.**

## What we collect

Nothing. SongSmash has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no servers, so there is
nowhere for your data to go.

## What stays on your device

Your game setup — team names, team colors, music filters, difficulty, and
target score — is saved only on your device so the app can remember it
between launches. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app
deletes this data.

## Apple Music

SongSmash finds songs and plays 30-second previews using Apple's own music
services (MusicKit / the Apple Music catalog). These requests go directly
from your device to Apple. They are governed by Apple's privacy policy
(https://www.apple.com/privacy/), not ours. SongSmash asks for Apple Music
access permission only so Apple's framework can determine your country's
music catalog; the app never reads your music library, playlists, or
listening history, and works without an Apple Music subscription.

## Children

SongSmash is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of SongSmash ever changes what data it handles, this
policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
