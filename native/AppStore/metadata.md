## Description (4000 chars max)

SongSmash turns any get-together into a music showdown.

Split into teams, choose your music, and listen: a mystery song starts
playing. Can your team name the song and the artist before the reveal? Tap
once if you got one of them, twice if you nailed both — first team to the
target score wins the night.

HOW IT WORKS
• Make 2 or more teams and pick a color for each
• Choose genres and decades — from 60s classics to this week's charts — or
  leave it open for everything
• Pick your difficulty: chart-topping hits, deeper cuts, or expert-level
  b-sides
• Listen to a 30-second preview, make your guesses, reveal the answer, and
  score with a tap
• First team to the target score (you choose — 25 points by default) wins,
  confetti included

MADE FOR GAME NIGHT
• One phone, pass it around — no accounts, no sign-ups, no subscription
  required
• Millions of real songs and previews from the Apple Music catalog
• Smart repeat protection keeps songs from coming back round after round —
  even across games
• Your teams and settings are remembered for next time
• Designed for the dark — a glassy, glowing look that feels great at a party

SongSmash uses Apple Music previews, so every clip is the real recording —
no covers, no karaoke versions. An internet connection is required. No Apple
Music subscription is needed to play.

Turn the volume up. Someone at the table knows this one.

## App Privacy questionnaire (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

Answer: **Data Not Collected** for every category.

Rationale you can paste if Apple asks: SongSmash has no accounts, no
analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. Game settings are stored only on
the device. Song previews and catalog searches go directly from the device
to Apple's own Apple Music/iTunes services; SongSmash operates no servers
and receives no user data.

## Review notes (App Review → Notes)

SongSmash is a local pass-the-phone party game. Music previews and catalog
metadata come from Apple Music via MusicKit (catalog-only; the app requests
Music authorization solely so MusicKit can resolve the storefront — no
library access, no subscription required). There are no accounts; no demo
credentials are needed. To test: add two teams on the setup screen, tap
Start Game, and allow Apple Music access when prompted.
