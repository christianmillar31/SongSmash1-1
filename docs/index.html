SongSmash

SongSmash is a pass-the-phone music guessing game. Add two or more teams, pick genres and decades, and guess songs from 30-second Apple Music previews.

Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email christianmillar31@gmail.com and include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Do I need an Apple Music subscription? No. SongSmash plays free 30-second catalog previews. The one-time Apple Music permission prompt only lets the app find songs for your country.

Does SongSmash collect my data? No. See the privacy policy — there are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.
